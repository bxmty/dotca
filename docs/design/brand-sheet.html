Every pairing passes WCAG 2 AA: 4.5:1 for text, 3:1 for large figures, button edges and the logo. Queen blue is for actions and evidence only: the CTA, prices, key figures, and focus, current and selected states. Step numerals and markers are ink or muted. Pale purple is only a surface, and the same surface in both modes. In dark mode it's a light island: a .hl scope switches ink, muted, rules and queen to their light values on it. Queen on pale purple is 4.13:1, so it's used only for figures 24 px and larger. Buttons stay off the island, so each mode has one CTA colour. In the footer band, focus rings use the band figure colour (6.80:1). Opal from the 2022 palette is not used.
