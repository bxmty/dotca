# Boximity client services

The language of the work Boximity does for its managed-IT clients, starting with joiner/leaver service requests.

## Language

### Service requests

**Service request**:
A client's request to onboard or offboard one employee, filed through the client's intake link.
_Avoid_: Intake, form submission

**Requester**:
The client's authorized contact who files a service request.
_Avoid_: Submitter, user

**New hire**:
The person a joiner service request onboards.
_Avoid_: New user, joiner (for the person), employee

**Departing employee**:
The person a leaver service request offboards.
_Avoid_: Leaver (for the person), terminated user

**Personal contact details**:
A new hire's own email address and phone number, separate from the work account Boximity creates for them.
_Avoid_: Contact info, private email, home phone
